On-Water Sequence

  1. Safety first. Forecast, lightning, visibility, fuel, PFDs, kill switch, VHF/phone.
  2. Read water. Wind → tide/current → clarity → bait → structure.
  3. Find life. Mullet, shrimp, birds, slicks, seams, sonar bait.
  4. Controlled drift. Upwind/upcurrent; shut the 250s down early.
  5. Mark every bite. Save depth/edge/waypoint and repeat the drift.
  6. Move rule. No bait/bites/signs in ~15–20 minutes = move.
